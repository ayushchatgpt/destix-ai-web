## 3. Engine-Specific GEO Mechanics: Technical Levers by Platform

Different AI engines rely on distinct retrieval pipelines. A single generic SEO strategy fails across multimodal engines.

### 1. Google (Organic Search, Local 3-Pack & AI Overviews)
- **Primary Mechanism:** Googlebot web indexing + Knowledge Graph entity reconciliation + Search Generative Experience (SGE).
- **Core Optimization Lever:** Exact-match semantic title tags (`"AI Voice Agents in [City] \| DestiX AI"`), JSON-LD `@graph` with `@id` cross-references to Wikidata `Q142174891`, and IndexNow instant pinging.
- **Defect Remediation:** Eliminating self-promotional "Best" claims prevented AI Overviews from filtering our pages as biased promotional copy.

### 2. ChatGPT Search (OpenAI)
- **Primary Mechanism:** Bing index retrieval + `OAI-SearchBot` real-time browsing + consensus synthesis from authoritative secondary directories.
- **Core Optimization Lever:** Dense markdown files (`public/llms.txt`), clear tabular comparisons, third-party verification profiles (Clutch, Crunchbase, GitHub), and high factual density without conversational filler words.

### 3. Perplexity AI
- **Primary Mechanism:** `PerplexityBot` live web scraping + multi-source citation aggregation + reciprocal link checking.
- **Core Optimization Lever:** Numerical claims paired with verifiable proof (e.g., citing the specific 524,000-call benchmark at `/research/2026-enterprise-ai-telephony-benchmark`, client URL `acpdubai.com`, and exact carrier wholesale rates). Perplexity favors sources with clear data points over prose-heavy explanations.

### 4. Claude (Anthropic)
- **Primary Mechanism:** Retrieval-augmented reasoning requiring search results before URL fetch.
- **Core Optimization Lever:** Search-optimized brand queries (`"DestiX AI ayush.company"`) and clear navigational anchor texts across GitHub, Wikidata, and LinkedIn.

### 5. Google Gemini
- **Primary Mechanism:** Direct access to Google's Knowledge Graph, Google Business Profile records, and YouTube multimodal transcript indexes.
- **Core Optimization Lever:** Verified Google Business Profile (Connaught Place, 110001), consistent business NAP (Name, Address, Phone), and schema markup linked to Google's internal entity IDs (`kgmid`).

### 6. YouTube Multimodal AEO
- **Primary Mechanism:** Ingestion of automated video captions, speech-to-text transcripts, and chapter metadata.
- **Action Plan:** Upload 90-second unedited screen captures of live calls across 4 accents (Delhi Hinglish, Dubai Gulf Arabic, London British RP, NYC American). Embed each video into its corresponding location page using Schema.org `VideoObject` with synchronized timestamps. When prospects ask Gemini or ChatGPT for proof of voice quality, the engine cites the verified video transcript.
